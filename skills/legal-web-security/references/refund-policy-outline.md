## Scope and product

Describe the products, subscriptions, services, digital goods, physical goods, trials, bundles, gift cards, and regions covered. Link the applicable Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy/Notice, and any service-specific order terms.

- Covered offers: <mark>[PRODUCTS/SERVICES]</mark>

- Digital-content or customized-work treatment: <mark>[DETAILS]</mark>

## Prices, renewals, and fees

State the price, currency, billing cadence, taxes or mandatory charges, trial conversion, renewal notice, cancellation deadline, payment method, and any variable amount before the customer commits. The policy must match the checkout total and receipt.

- Price and currency: <mark>[AMOUNT/CURRENCY]</mark>
- Recurring amount and frequency: <mark>[DETAILS OR NOT APPLICABLE]</mark>
- Mandatory fees, taxes, shipping, or usage charges: <mark>[DETAILS]</mark>
- Trial and automatic-renewal terms: <mark>[DETAILS OR NOT APPLICABLE]</mark>

## Eligibility and refund options

State the actual eligibility conditions, any time limits, prorating method, proof needed, treatment of partially used services, failed delivery, duplicate charges, unauthorized charges, and statutory or contractual rights that cannot be waived.

- Eligible events: <mark>[EVENTS]</mark>
- Request window and time zone: <mark>[WINDOW]</mark>
- Proration/partial use: <mark>[RULE]</mark>
- Non-refundable items and reason: <mark>[ITEMS AND APPROVED BASIS]</mark>
- Consumer/statutory rights reservation: <mark>[COUNSEL-APPROVED TEXT]</mark>

## How to cancel or request a refund

Explain the steps in plain language. The cancellation/refund path should be at least as easy to find and use as the purchase path unless counsel approves a lawful alternative.

1. Go to <mark>[ACCOUNT/REQUEST URL]</mark> or contact <mark>[CHANNEL]</mark>.
2. Provide <mark>[MINIMUM INFORMATION NEEDED TO LOCATE THE TRANSACTION]</mark>.
3. Receive a confirmation/reference number: <mark>[PROCESS]</mark>.
4. Expected decision and payment timing: <mark>[TIMEFRAME/METHOD]</mark>.

Do not require unnecessary personal data or make a customer call, navigate a maze, or accept an upsell merely to cancel unless that requirement is specifically approved and lawful.
